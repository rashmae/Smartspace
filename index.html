Unified Campus Facility Reservation & Availability Checking

Designed to reduce double bookings, misplaced paper requisition slips, and scheduling confusion. SmartSpace gives academic departments, faculty organizers, and student organizations visibility into venue schedules, capacity limits, equipment summaries, and custodial buffers.

  • Conflict-Checked Scheduling: Checks requested time blocks against existing reservations to help prevent overlaps.
  • Multi-Tier Approvals: Configurable review steps for advisers, department chairs, facility administrators, and security staff.
  • Digital QR Gate Pass: Scannable reservation pass for campus custodians and guards on event day.
  • Utilization & Audit Records: Track reservation history and room usage across configured campus facilities.

How SmartSpace Works: 5-Step Booking Lifecycle

  1. Discover & Filter: Browse campus auditoriums, gyms, laboratories, and lecture rooms with capacity, rules, and equipment details.
  2. Submit Reservation: Choose your date, time block, event title, expected headcount, and required endorsement details.
  3. Institutional Clearance: Structured review workflow through configured academic and physical plant approvers.
  4. Digital QR Gate Pass: Generate a reservation QR code and verification reference upon approval.
  5. Campus Security Check-in: Building custodians scan or check the pass at the venue entrance to confirm reservation details.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does SmartSpace help prevent overlapping bookings across university venues?

SmartSpace checks proposed timeslots against existing approved and pending reservations in the configured schedule so administrators and requesters can spot overlaps before confirmation.

Can student organizations request facilities across configured campus workspaces?

Yes. Each participating institution workspace defines its own booking clearance rules and endorsement steps, allowing authorized users to submit requests according to host rules.

How are physical campus gates and check-ins supported on event day?

Once an administrator approves a reservation, SmartSpace generates a digital QR gate pass code that campus custodians and security staff can inspect on a mobile device to verify reservation details.

What equipment and operating rules can administrators configure?

Administrators can configure advance notice expectations, required endorsement notes, maximum room capacities, equipment inventories, and operating hours per facility.

How can a college or university try the SmartSpace pilot?

SmartSpace is currently a pilot-stage prototype. Higher-education teams in Cebu City can request a guided walkthrough or join the pilot to evaluate the workflow with sample campus spaces.
