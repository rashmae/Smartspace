**Disclaimer:** This register was prepared during an engineering and trust audit for SmartSpace (target market: higher-education institutions in Cebu City, Philippines). It is not legal advice; all legal documents, compliance determinations, and Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), Consumer Act (RA 7394), and Internet Transactions Act (RA 11967) obligations must be reviewed by a qualified Philippine lawyer prior to commercial launch.

## Inventory of `[TO BE CONFIRMED: ...]` Placeholders Needed From Owner & Counsel

1. `legalEntityName` — Registered sole proprietorship, partnership, or corporation name.
2. `businessRegistrationNo` — DTI Business Name Certificate number or SEC Registration / TIN details.
3. `registeredAddress` — Official business address in Cebu City, Philippines.
4. `supportEmail` — Monitored support & general inquiries email address.
5. `supportPhone` — Monitored official business telephone/mobile number.
6. `dpoName` — Designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) under RA 10173.
7. `dpoEmail` — Direct DPO contact email for data subject rights requests.
8. `npcRegistrationStatus` — National Privacy Commission (NPC) Personal Information Controller (PIC) / DPO registration status.
9. `domainOwnership` — Verified production domain ownership and canonical host.
10. `effectiveDate` — Official effective date once counsel approves the legal pages.
11. `governingLawVenue` — Exact venue clause (e.g., proper courts of Cebu City) reviewed by counsel.
12. `hostingProvider` — Production hosting vendor and region(s) alongside Google Firebase (`ai-studio-smartspace-d526faed-3a75-4ead-a7c7-c9666ec1c436`; Case Study 6 notes AWS/Vercel/Heroku are under evaluation).
13. `paymentMethods` — Verified institutional payment methods (`billingCycle` filled as monthly proposed institutional subscription from Case Study 6; commercial invoicing and gateway vendor still pending selection).
14. `refundEligibility`, `partialMonthRefunds`, `refundResponseTime`, `refundProcessingTime` — Commercial refund rules compliant with RA 7394 & RA 11967.
15. `retentionPeriods` — Specific retention schedules for demo inquiries, user accounts, booking/audit logs, and consent records.
16. `accessibilityResponseTime` — Target SLA for responding to reported accessibility barriers.
17. **Written permission** from each prospective student team member (`Ansay, Rash Mae Crystelle`; `Coloyan, Karl Hadriel`; `Gonzales, Angel Mae`; `Miñoza, Krisha`) if a "Team" section is to be shown publicly.
18. **Owner approval requested** before making structural changes to `firestore.rules`, `server.ts` authentication/allowlist logic, or moving PII out of browser `localStorage` (`RISK-02`, `RISK-03`, `RISK-04`).
