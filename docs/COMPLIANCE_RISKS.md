# SmartSpace Legal, Privacy, Security, and Accessibility Risk Register

**Disclaimer:** This register was prepared during an engineering and trust audit for SmartSpace (target market: higher-education institutions in Cebu City, Philippines). It is not legal advice; all legal documents, compliance determinations, and Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), Consumer Act (RA 7394), and Internet Transactions Act (RA 11967) obligations must be reviewed by a qualified Philippine lawyer prior to commercial launch.

**Audit Baseline Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Active Remediation Workflow:** Tracked across Sections 1–9 of the cleanup plan plus Owner Final Answers applied across `src/config/business.ts`, public pages, legal pages, metadata, and sitemaps. Items affecting `firestore.rules`, authentication logic, `server.ts`, or core data structures are staged for one-change-at-a-time execution upon owner confirmation.
